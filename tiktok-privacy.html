Privacy Policy (TikTok)

Last updated: 28 September 2026

What the app does

VeraCuenta Uploader is a desktop tool used only by the creator of the VeraCuenta channel. It sends the channel's own videos to the creator's TikTok inbox as drafts. The creator reviews and publishes every video in the TikTok app.

Data we receive

When the creator authorizes the app with TikTok Login Kit, TikTok provides an access token, a refresh token, the granted scopes and the TikTok user ID (open_id). The app requests only the user.info.basic and video.upload scopes.

How we use it

This data is used only to upload the creator's own videos as drafts and to check the status of each upload. It is stored only on the creator's own computer. It is not sent to any other server, sold, shared or used for advertising.

Data we do not collect

The app does not read or store videos from other users, messages, followers, comments, analytics or any other personal data.

Retention and removal

Tokens are kept until they expire or until access is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services, or equivalent), which makes the stored tokens useless.
