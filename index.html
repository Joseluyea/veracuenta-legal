VeraCuenta Uploader

VeraCuenta Uploader is the desktop tool of the VeraCuenta channel, which publishes original, dramatized first-person stories narrated by Vera on YouTube and TikTok.

On TikTok, it sends the channel's own videos to the creator's TikTok inbox as drafts through TikTok's official Content Posting API; the creator reviews, edits and publishes each video in the TikTok app. On YouTube, it uploads the channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API.
